<yellow><item 'polyfactory:redstone_valve_pipe'></> is a block that allows you to easily disconnect pipes from each other conditionally.
<nl2>
By default, it will allow fluid to pass unless you power it with Redstone. When so, fluids won't be able to flow in/out of this
pipe and any fluid flow generated with a <pageref './mechanical_pump'> or other blocks will be cut out from the other side.
<nl>
You can configure this block with a <pageref '/basics/wrench'>, allowing you to invert its Redstone interaction.
<nl2>
<item 'polyfactory:redstone_valve_pipe'> act similarly to pipes, being able to pull, push and store fluids within them.
