<yellow><item 'polyfactory:trommel'></> is an 2x1x1 item crafting machine block. It required rotational energy to work.
It has one input slot and 5 output slots.
<nl2>
This machine allows you to break down select items into different ones. Result of the input item can range
from nothing to few items in different quantities, as result is chance based.
<nl2>
To power this block you need to connect its front or back to some rotational energy transmission blocks.
The stress generated by this machine depends on currently crafted recipe and speed at which it's currently rotated.
When inactive it always requires 1 stress unit.
<nl2>
To input items into it, connect a <pageref '/item_transport/funnel'> or a Hopper anywhere into the back of the block.
To pull out items connect them to the front block instead. The front/back are indicated with an arrow texture on top and bottom
of the block, with it pointing from input to output.
