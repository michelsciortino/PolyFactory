- Added Copper Pipe with Smart Valve:
  - It's an automated version of the Redstone Valve, that closes automatically once selected amount of fluid passes through it.
  - It can be programmed with a Wrench or by connecting Data Cable to it.
  - The limit is reset once this block gets powered with Redstone or when new configuration is received via data network.
  - Crafted with Copper Pipe with Redstone Valve, 2 Steel Plates, a Cable and a Redstone Chip.
- Doubled amount of items a single Item Filter can check for (from 9 to 18)
- Canisters now allow for more interactions:
  - In world fluid insertion (on use) and extraction (on attack), using same block conversion as pipes.
  - You can now use the Canister to milk cows.
  - Canisters can now be filled with Mechanical Spouts and emptied with Mechanical Drains.
