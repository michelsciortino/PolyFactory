- Added Copper Pipe with Smart Valve:
  - It's an automated version of the Redstone Valve, that closes automatically once selected amount of fluid passes through it.
  - It can be programmed with a Wrench or by connecting Data Cable to it.
  - The limit is reset once this block gets powered with Redstone or when new configuration is received via data network.
  - Crafted with Copper Pipe with Redstone Valve, 2 Steel Plates, a Cable and a Redstone Chip.
- Added Trommel:
  - A new machine block, used to separate out materials from blocks/items such as Gravel and Dirt.
  - It's 1x1x2 (flat), with the back being used for input and front for output (see arrows on top/bottom).
  - To work it needs to be powered with rotational energy in front or back.
  - Crafted with 5 Steel Plates, 2 Smooth Stone Slabs, a Steel Machine Gearbox and a Metal Grid.
- Added Configurator:
  - A new data receiver block that can be used to change the configuration of the block it faces.
  - Think about it as Wrench controlled via received data.
  - The format it takes is `type value`, where type is id of the setting and value is the value it should be set to.
  - By interacting with it while not holding any item the ui displaying accepted setting types and their values are displayed.
  - Crafted with 4 Steel Plates, 3 Copper Ingots, Redstone Chip and a Wrench.
- Added Liquid Fertilizer:
  - Bone Meal in a liquid form, which can be used with Fluid Nozzle and Pressure Fluid Gun.
  - Crafted in a Mixer with Water, Bone Meal and 2 Biomass.
- Canisters now allow for more interactions:
  - In world fluid insertion (on use) and extraction (on attack), using same block conversion as pipes.
  - You can now use the Canister to milk cows.
  - Canisters can now be filled with Mechanical Spouts and emptied with Mechanical Drains.
  - Canisters can now be used to select filtered fluid in Filtered Copper Pipe.
- Drills in 2x2 and 3x3 modes now get an extra mining speed bonus for blocks that would insta-mine otherwise.
- Doubled amount of items a single Item Filter can check for (from 9 to 18).
- All double-block-tall and Grinder machines can now be rotated with a Wrench.
- Item Filters can now be used with Hopper Minecarts (same as regular Hoppers).
- Attacking with Pressure Fluid Gun will now shoot out a burst of fluids (equal to 3 ticks of regular usage) with extra force and spread.
- Lava Splashes (Lava shoot out of a Nozzle / Pressure Fluid Gun) can now burn off grass and alike, rather than being prevented by it.
- Changed the look of Clutch and Copper Pipe with Redstone Valve to mirror one of Gated Cable.
  - As such, Copper Pipe with Redstone Valve now changes it's texture when powered.
- Changed recipes of Clutch and Copper Pipe with Redstone Valve to use Smooth Stone Slabs instead of Steel Plates.
- Changed Mechanical Spout and Mechanical Drain recipes to take less time (30% less).
- Grinder can now store 5 stacks of output items (was 3 before).
- Fluids added by other mods should now be automatically compatible with PolyFactory (no transfer compatibility yet through).
- Increased amount of Plant Oil received from seeds.
- Updated Creative Motor item model to match the look of the in world one (same as change in previous update).
- Conveyors can now put items into composters (as it was originally intended, but didn't work due to a bug).
- Tweaked Grinder textures to make it visible where the front/input and back/output is.
- Machine State will now distinctly say if it's too slow due to not being connected to a source.
- Funnels can now pull out items out of Composters.
- New recipes:
  - Fermenting all Flowers, all Saplings (aside azalea), all Grass, Moss Carpets, Pale Hanging Moss, Kelp and Leaves creates 1 Biomass at 10% chance.
  - Fermenting Azaleas, all Moss and Cactus create 1 Biomass at 40% chance.
  - Fermenting Pumpkin creates 1 Biomass at 60% chance, 1-2 Pumpkin Seeds and Ethanol.
  - Fermenting Melon Block creates 1 biomass at 50% chance, 3-4 Melon Seeds and a little bit of Ethanol.
  - Fermenting Melon Slice creates 1 biomass at 5% chance.
